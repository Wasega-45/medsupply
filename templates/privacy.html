Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Our Approach to Privacy

MedSupply Kenya is a coordination platform for medical supplies and delivery logistics. We deliberately collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the system. We do not collect patient-identifiable information.

2. What We Collect

  • User information: name, email, role, phone (if provided), account status
  • Facility information: facility name, type, county, location, GPS coordinates, contact
  • Supply hub information: hub name, county, location, GPS coordinates, service radius
  • Order data: medicines requested, quantities, urgency, timestamps, status
  • Delivery data: origin hub, destination facility, method, status, timestamps
  • Inventory data: stock levels, reserved quantities, update timestamps

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Patient names, IDs, or medical records
  • Patient diagnoses or prescriptions
  • Payment or financial information
  • Browsing history or tracking cookies for advertising
  • Any data not listed in Section 2 above

4. How We Use the Data

Collected data is used only to:

  • Process and fulfil supply requests
  • Manage inventory and coordinate deliveries
  • Display facilities, hubs, and coverage on the GIS map
  • Calculate distances and service-area coverage
  • Generate operational reports for authorised users
  • Maintain audit records for accountability

5. Who Can See Your Data

Access is strictly role-based:

  • Facility users — can see their own facility's information and orders
  • Logistics officers — can see orders, inventory, and delivery data
  • County administrators — can see data within their county
  • National administrators — can see system-wide data
  • Monitoring users — can view dashboards and maps according to their assigned permissions

Users only see what their role permits. No user can access data outside their authorisation.

6. Data Security

  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard cryptographic hashing — never in plain text
  • All access to the System requires authentication
  • HTTPS encryption is used for all deployed traffic
  • User input is validated to prevent injection and other common attacks
  • Important actions are logged for audit purposes

7. Data Retention

Operational data (orders, deliveries, inventory records) is retained for auditing, reporting, and historical analysis. User accounts can be deactivated when they are no longer needed. When the system is deployed in production, retention periods will follow applicable Kenyan health-sector and data-protection regulations.

8. Your Rights

As an authorised user, you may:

  • Request to see the account information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request that your account be deactivated

Contact your system administrator for any of these requests.

9. Drone and Location Data

Drone position data and facility/hub coordinates are treated as operationally sensitive. Real drone telemetry is only integrated through an authorised service. Live location data is never shared publicly.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the system administrator.
