About MedSupply Kenya

Real-Time GIS-Based Medical Supply and Drone Delivery Management System

What is MedSupply Kenya?

MedSupply Kenya is a web-based platform that helps health facilities across Kenyan counties request medical supplies, manage inventory, and coordinate delivery logistics — including drone-supported delivery where an authorised service is available.

Who is it for?

  • Health facilities — submit supply requests and track progress
  • Supply and logistics officers — manage inventory, approve requests, coordinate deliveries
  • County administrators — manage county data, users, and dashboards
  • National administrators — oversee all counties and system-wide settings
  • Monitoring operators — view active deliveries, maps, and alerts

What the system does

  • Records supply requests from health facilities in real time
  • Manages inventory, stock levels, and reservations
  • Uses GIS mapping to display facilities, supply hubs, and coverage areas
  • Calculates distances, service coverage, and identifies underserved areas
  • Tracks delivery status from approval to receipt
  • Supports drone delivery monitoring where an authorised integration is available
  • Provides dashboards for county and national oversight

What the system does NOT do

  • It does not provide medical advice or clinical decisions
  • It does not store patient-identifiable information
  • It does not directly control drones — only monitors authorised telemetry where available
  • It supports qualified clinical, logistics, and aviation decisions — it does not replace them

Multi-County Design

The platform is designed to support all 47 Kenyan counties. Kisumu is currently used as a pilot dataset. Any county can be added by registering its facilities, hubs, and administrative boundaries as configuration data — no code changes are required.
