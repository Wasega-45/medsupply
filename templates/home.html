About the System

MedSupply Kenya is a web-based platform that helps health facilities across Kenyan counties request medical supplies, manage inventory, and coordinate delivery logistics — including drone-supported delivery where an authorised service is available.

Use the sidebar to navigate the system. Authorised users can view maps, place orders, track deliveries, and analyse coverage.
